## How Does the 4-Step Pipeline Actually Work?

Here's my exact workflow for a 3–5 minute explainer video. Total time: 2–3 hours from brief to delivery.

**Step 1: Script (Claude + 20 minutes of editing)**

The client sends a one-paragraph brief. I paste it into Claude with this prompt structure: "Write a 450-word explainer video script for [company], covering [topic]. Target audience: [description]. Tone: professional but approachable. No industry jargon. End with a clear call to action."

First draft is usually 80% there. I spend 15–20 minutes editing for accuracy and matching the client's brand voice.

**Step 2: Voice (ElevenLabs, 5 minutes)**

Paste the script into ElevenLabs. Select the saved voice profile for that client. Generate and download the MP3.

That's it. Literally five minutes.

**Step 3: Video (Synthesia, export takes 15–20 minutes)**

Open Synthesia, create a new project, paste the script. Select the avatar we set up during client onboarding. Add any slide backgrounds or B-roll the client provided. Hit export.

For clients who don't want an avatar — just narration over screen recordings or slides — I skip Synthesia entirely. ElevenLabs audio + basic screencasting software gets the job done at half the cost.

**Step 4: Deliver**

Finished MP4 goes into their Google Drive folder. Short delivery note with timestamps for any sections they might want to revise. Invoice sent.

Some clients pay an extra $200/month for me to handle social publishing too. Easy add-on.

## Who Is This Model Actually Right For?

This works well if you:
- Can write or edit clear, audience-appropriate scripts
- Are comfortable navigating basic SaaS dashboards
- Have patience for the 4–6 week client acquisition ramp
- Don't need income to start on day one

My background in tech made the B2B client outreach easier — I had an existing LinkedIn network in fintech and SaaS. If you're starting from scratch, budget 8–10 weeks instead of 6 before your first retainer client.

## What Are the Real Risks?

**Platform dependency.** If ElevenLabs or Synthesia raises prices dramatically, changes their terms, or goes under, my cost structure breaks. I keep three months of income in cash as a buffer and I'm testing HeyGen as a Synthesia backup.

**AI detection.** Clients are starting to ask whether their audience will recognize AI-generated content. My honest answer: some will. Platforms are adding AI labels. I focus on segments where transparency isn't a dealbreaker — internal training videos, B2B explainers, not consumer-facing brand content.

**Client churn.** All three retainer clients could leave in the same month. It's happened before in freelancing. I keep my Upwork profile active even when full so I'm never scrambling from zero.

**Income classification.** This is active work, not passive income in the strict sense. April's $4,247 took approximately 35 hours of actual work. That's $121/hour — excellent. But "passive" is the wrong word. I call it freedom income: I work from a café in Bali on a schedule I set, not a commute I inherit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need video editing experience?**

No. The ElevenLabs and Synthesia interfaces are designed for non-technical users. The pipeline I described doesn't touch traditional editing software. If a client requests specific animation or complex graphics, I outsource that to a freelancer and factor the cost into my pricing.

**Q: What does ElevenLabs actually cost in 2026?**

As of May 2026, approximately: Starter ($5/month, limited characters), Creator ($22/month, ~100K chars), Pro ($99/month, ~500K chars). Pricing changes — check their website directly. The Creator plan covers most freelance use cases.

**Q: How do I find my first client?**

Best channels for me: LinkedIn outreach to founders and marketing managers at B2B companies, Upwork postings under "explainer video" or "corporate video production," and direct cold outreach to companies whose websites clearly lack video content. First client usually takes 2–4 weeks of active daily outreach.

**Q: Is AI-generated video legal to sell as a commercial service?**

As of May 2026, yes — subject to each platform's terms of service. Both ElevenLabs and Synthesia explicitly allow commercial use on paid plans. Always verify current terms before signing client contracts, as ToS documents change.

PlayHT and Murf are the main alternatives I've tested. ElevenLabs has the most natural-sounding voices in my testing as of early 2026. Murf is simpler and cheaper for basic narration. PlayHT has strong API options for automation workflows.
