## What Is Bybit Earn?

Bybit Earn is the exchange's umbrella dashboard for yield products. Deposit crypto, pick a product: flexible savings (withdraw anytime), fixed-term locked deposits, liquid ETH or SOL staking, or Launchpool (stake to earn new token allocations pre-listing).

It's centralized yield. You're trusting Bybit's solvency and security — not a smart contract. The tradeoff is zero gas fees, no self-custody complexity, and a familiar exchange interface.

## The 4 Products I Tested: Real Results

I ran this test February through April 2026 with $5,000 split across all four product types. Every number below is from my own account records.

### 1. Flexible USDT Savings

**Advertised rate:** Up to 11% APR  
**What I actually earned:** 8.2%–11% APR on the first ~$190, then 3.1% on everything above

This is Bybit's biggest marketing sleight of hand. The headline rate is real — for a tiny promotional threshold of roughly $190–$200 equivalent. Everything above earns the base floating rate, which sat at 3.1%–3.2% APR throughout my test window.

My blended effective yield on a $1,000 USDT position: approximately 3.4% APR.

That's not embarrassing — it's competitive with what most bank high-yield accounts were offering in early 2026. But it's a long way from 11%.

**Bottom line:** The promo rate is a welcome bonus on your first deposit, not a yield strategy for real capital.

### 2. Fixed-Term USDT (7-day and 30-day)

**Advertised rate:** Up to 15% APR  
**What I earned:** 10.8%–11.4% APR on 7-day products, 9.2%–9.8% on 30-day

This genuinely surprised me. The 7-day fixed products delivered real yields well above the flexible rate — and above what Binance and OKX were offering on comparable products during the same period.

The catch: inventory moves fast. I'd check at 9am UTC on product refresh days (typically Mondays and Thursdays) and watch the $500-minimum slots disappear within two hours.

My system: Google Calendar alerts for 7:45am UTC on refresh days. Got in four out of six times over 90 days.

**Bottom line:** 7-day fixed is the best product on the platform — if you can actually access it.

### 3. ETH Liquid Staking

**Advertised rate:** 3.5%–4.2% APY  
**What I earned:** 3.1%–3.8% APY (as of May 2026, APY fluctuates with network conditions)

Bybit wraps your ETH into a liquid staking token that earns validator rewards. Unstaking takes 2–4 days. No gas fees. No Metamask required.

The rates land slightly below what [Lido and Rocket Pool](/blog/lido-vs-rocket-pool-vs-eigenlayer-2026) were offering during the same period through DeFi — but for someone already holding ETH on Bybit, the friction cost of moving to DeFi ate into that difference.

**Confession moment:** I ran the math on moving 1.2 ETH to Lido directly. After gas, the break-even point was 11 months. I stayed on Bybit and kept the liquidity.

For long-term ETH holders: this is close to free money on an asset you'd hold anyway. Not spectacular, but consistent.

**Bottom line:** Best Bybit Earn product for existing ETH holders. Zero friction.

### 4. Launchpool

**Advertised rate:** 50%–300%+ APR (token rewards before listing)  
**What I earned:** -12% net (yes, negative)

I staked BIT tokens for a Q1 2026 Launchpool campaign. The new token launched, I claimed my allocation — then watched the token dump 38% in the first six hours of trading. Staking rewards didn't cover the unrealized loss on my BIT position.

I know people who tripled their position from a single Launchpool in early 2026. I was not one of them. That's the game.

**Bottom line:** Cap Launchpool exposure at 3%–5% of your Bybit portfolio. Treat it as speculative, not passive income.
