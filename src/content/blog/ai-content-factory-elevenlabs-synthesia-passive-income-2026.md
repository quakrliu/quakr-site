## Step 1: The Script (20 Minutes Per Video)

I use Claude for scripts now. My prompt template: topic + target length (8–12 minutes) + three audience frustrations the video should address.

Confession: my first 11 videos were clearly AI-written and it showed. Comments said "this sounds robotic." I spent two weeks figuring out how to get a more grounded tone — and the fix wasn't a better AI. It was me manually rewriting the opening 30 seconds and adding one story from my actual life. Everything else I leave as-is.

An example: my video on stablecoin yields after the CLARITY Act started ranking because I added this line: "My neighbor in Seminyak asked me last week why his Aave rate dropped from 11% to 4%." That's a real question from a real person. The AI didn't write that sentence. I did.

Scripts run about 1,200–1,800 words per 10-minute video.

## The Risks Worth Knowing

**YouTube 2026 policy:** Earlier this year, thousands of mass-produced AI channels had monetization suspended. The rule isn't "no AI content" — it's "no low-effort, high-volume, identical content." Four focused videos per week with original research is fine. Forty identical videos generated from one prompt is not. YouTube requires disclosure for AI-generated content in certain categories — read their creator guidelines before you publish.

**ElevenLabs plan matters:** The $5/month Starter plan sounds noticeably worse than Creator for a YouTube audience. Pay the $22. It's the difference between content people finish and content they close after 90 seconds.

**Synthesia's 10-minute monthly cap:** At 4 videos of 2–3 minutes each, Starter works. If you want long-form 10-minute videos from day one, budget for the Creator plan ($67/month) — that changes your payback timeline.

**Niche saturation:** This works because I'm specific. If a better channel covers your exact topic better than you do, views migrate. Stay narrow, stay updated, and actually respond to your audience's questions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does it cost to start an AI content factory?**

Minimum viable setup: $51/month — ElevenLabs Creator ($22) plus Synthesia Starter ($29). Adding Canva Pro, a scheduling tool, and email marketing brings it to $80–$120/month. You don't need all of these on day one.

**How long before an AI YouTube channel makes real money?**

In high-RPM niches (finance, software, B2B), expect 4–6 months to reach your first $100/month. Reaching $1,000/month typically takes 8–12 months of consistent 4-videos-per-week output. Broad niches take longer or plateau early.

**Is ElevenLabs voice quality good enough for YouTube?**

On the Creator plan with Professional Voice Cloning, yes. 192 kbps audio is broadcast quality. Several channels with 100K+ subscribers use voice cloning — audiences care about useful information, not who's talking.

**Does YouTube allow AI-generated videos in 2026?**

Yes. YouTube's policy targets low-effort mass production, not AI use itself. A focused channel publishing 3–4 research-backed videos per week is within guidelines. Disclosure may be required for certain categories under YouTube's AI content policy.

**What affiliate programs work best for finance YouTube channels?**

In my experience: crypto tax software (CoinLedger pays $30–$60 per conversion), email tools (Beehiiv), and voice/video AI tools (ElevenLabs has its own referral program). Finance audiences are high-intent buyers — a clear recommendation in a video description converts better than you'd expect.
